# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release, for Minecraft 26.3.

- Building, path, floor and shape palettes from a base block, theme and mood.
- 8 themes and 6 moods.
- House, path, floor and 3D shape previews.
- Around 75 floor patterns; 8 shapes from 5 to 126 blocks with a layer-by-layer view.
- Score breakdown for every block, swap options, locks and reroll.
- Decor ideas for buildings.
- Saved palettes and `pp1:` share codes.
- Blocks from other mods are picked up automatically.
- Give to hotbar in Creative, gather checklist in Survival.
